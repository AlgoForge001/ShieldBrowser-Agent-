Why This Matters for Autonomous AI Agents

✓
Zero-Knowledge Privacy Pipeline

Ordinary AI browser agents upload raw full-page screenshots directly to OpenAI, Anthropic, or Gemini. ShieldBrowser guarantees that no biometric facial data ever leaves client RAM.

✓
Deterministic Mathematical Pixelation

Face regions are converted into 16-24px pixel block averages with randomized edge noise. It is mathematically impossible for an AI model or adversary to reconstruct the human identity.

Unbroken Agent Task Performance

The agent can still read buttons, navigate links, and extract article summaries without ever knowing the face or identity of the person on screen.
